Burrow Privacy Policy

Burrow: File Manager, by BoriSoft · Effective 27 September 2026

In short: Burrow does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Your files stay on your device. The Android app does not even have permission to use the internet.

What Burrow does with your files

Burrow reads, lists, opens, copies, moves, renames, deletes and extracts files on your device, and only when you ask it to. It builds an index of file names, sizes and dates so it can show categories, recent files and search results. That index is kept in the app's memory on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. Burrow has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. We have no servers that receive information from the app.

Your settings (theme, accent color, grid view, hidden files) are saved on your device only, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

PermissionWhy Burrow needs it
All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) and storage/media access To browse, search, open and manage the files on your device's storage and SD cards. This is the core purpose of a file manager.
Install unknown apps (REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES) To hand an APK file that you tap to Android's system installer. Burrow never installs anything on its own.
Keep awake (WAKE_LOCK) and network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Used by the built-in audio and video player: to keep music playing with the screen off, and by Android's media player library. Burrow still has no internet permission and cannot send anything off your device.
Photos and camera (iOS) Only to import photos or videos that you pick into Burrow's folder.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device settings.

Opening files in other apps

When you open a file that Burrow can't display itself, it is handed to another app you choose (for example a video player). What that app does with the file is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Web version

In the web version, files you import stay in your browser tab's memory and disappear when you close the tab. They are not uploaded.

Children

Burrow collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
